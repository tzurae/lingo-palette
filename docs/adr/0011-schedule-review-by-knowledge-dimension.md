# Schedule review by knowledge dimension

Each learning item maintains review schedules and evidence separately for five knowledge dimensions—contextual meaning, usage fit, grammar pattern, collocation, and optionally productive use—rather than collapsing them into one due date or mastery score. A review judgment changes only the dimension the review item actually measured; new encounters add context but do not count as successful retrieval, and ungradable responses leave scheduling unchanged. This prevents strong meaning recognition from hiding weak usage knowledge and lets review sessions target the due dimensions with the thinnest evidence.

Micro-review is outside the first release. Re-encountering a Learning Item records another Encounter without presenting retrieval during the Reading Flow and without advancing any schedule. First-release Review Evidence comes only from a Learner-initiated Review Session.

Productive-use Intent is configured per Learning Item in Saved, defaults off, and is never requested during saving. Enabling it creates or resumes that item's productive-use schedule. Disabling it pauses future productive prompts without deleting Review Evidence or the prior stage; re-enabling preserves the stage and makes an overdue schedule immediately due.
