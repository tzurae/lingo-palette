# Schedule review by knowledge dimension

Each learning item maintains review schedules and evidence separately for five knowledge dimensions—contextual meaning, usage fit, grammar pattern, collocation, and optionally productive use—rather than collapsing them into one due date or mastery score. A review judgment changes only the dimension the review item actually measured; new encounters add context but do not count as successful retrieval, and ungradable responses leave scheduling unchanged. This prevents strong meaning recognition from hiding weak usage knowledge and lets review sessions target the due dimensions with the thinnest evidence.

Micro-review is outside the first release. Re-encountering a Learning Item records another Encounter without presenting retrieval during the Reading Flow and without advancing any schedule. First-release Review Evidence comes only from a Learner-initiated Review Session.

Productive-use Intent is configured per Learning Item in Saved, defaults off, and is never requested during saving. Enabling it creates or resumes that item's productive-use schedule. Disabling it pauses future productive prompts without deleting Review Evidence or the prior stage; re-enabling preserves the stage and makes an overdue schedule immediately due.

The first durable scheduler seam stores versioned Review Schedule records independently from approved Review Items and Review Sessions. A schedule is keyed by stable Learning Item ID plus Knowledge Dimension and exposes `dueAt`, demonstrated-evidence count, and interval stage for inspection. Starting a session considers only active Learning Items whose approved item is due and not awaiting Evidence Pack revalidation. It ranks by earliest due time, thinnest demonstrated evidence, lowest interval stage, earliest Learning Item creation, stable Learning Item ID, Knowledge Dimension, and Review Item ID; it then keeps only the highest-ranked item per Learning Item and takes at most five.

Review Session progress is durable and reveal-first. The selected Review Item IDs, current position, and revealed IDs survive service-worker suspension and browser restart; accepted answers, distractors, and corrective explanation do not cross the UI boundary until the Learner explicitly reveals the current item. One to four eligible Learning Items produce an explicitly shortened session, while zero does not start. Completing this retrieval sequence does not itself create Review Evidence or change a schedule; the judgment and transition rules remain the responsibility of the Review Evidence flow.
