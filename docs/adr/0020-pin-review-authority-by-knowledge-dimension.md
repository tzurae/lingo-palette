# Pin review authority by knowledge dimension

Usage-fit and grammar-pattern Review Items are approved only by evidence whose authority matches the measured Knowledge Dimension, and each approved item carries a dimension-specific Source Authority snapshot that is copied unchanged into its Review Evidence. Usage fit requires matching sense/context evidence; lexical relation or frequency alone cannot establish contextual suitability. Grammar pattern requires a source-recorded grammatical frame or usage note, or repeated, POS-aware corpus attestations; an unsupported universal rule is rejected rather than taught. Missing, conflicting, cross-dimension, or insufficient evidence causes abstention or a grounded fallback, never approval of the disputed claim.

This first-release cutover extends the existing contextual-meaning Review path without combining dimensions: candidates declare exactly one measured dimension, schedules and calibration histories remain independent by Learning Item and dimension, and one dimension's answer cannot update another dimension. Pinning the authority snapshot makes later Evidence Pack revalidation and reviewer inspection reproducible even after the active pack changes.
