# Use an inspectable review-schedule baseline

The first release targets six-month-plus reading retention with a per-dimension interval ladder of 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 120, and 180 days, followed by low-frequency maintenance rather than permanent mastery. Review judgments move only the measured dimension: demonstrated advances, partial and acceptable-alternative responses receive earlier confirmation, not-demonstrated retreats, and unable-to-grade leaves the schedule untouched; self-reported evidence cannot reach long maintenance without objective calibration. This ladder is an explicit product baseline, not a claim of universal optimality, and any adaptive replacement must beat it on delayed outcomes through the evaluation loop.

A Review Session contains up to five approved, due Review Items with no repeated Learning Item. When only one to four unique due items exist, the Learner may start a shortened session that states the available count; the scheduler never pulls a not-yet-due item forward merely to reach five.

Session selection uses a deterministic lexicographic rank: earliest `dueAt`, then fewest demonstrated Review Evidence records for the measured Knowledge Dimension, then lowest interval stage, then earliest Learning Item creation time, then stable Learning Item ID, Knowledge Dimension, and Review Item ID. “Thinnest evidence” means the fewest demonstrated records; Encounters, partial judgments, and acceptable alternatives are not positive target evidence. Only the highest-ranked due Review Item for each Learning Item remains eligible before the first five are taken.

Only an objectively scored demonstrated judgment advances one interval stage. Objective acceptable-alternative and partial judgments retreat one stage, not-demonstrated resets to the one-day stage, and unable-to-grade changes neither stage nor due time. Self-assessed recalled-fluently keeps the stage and schedules its current interval from the attempt, recalled-with-effort retreats one stage, and did-not-recall resets to one day. Self-assessment therefore maintains or brings review earlier but never supplies positive target evidence.

An objectively demonstrated review at the 180-day stage enters a 365-day maintenance stage. Further demonstrated judgments or recalled-fluently self-assessments retain the 365-day interval. An acceptable-alternative, partial, or recalled-with-effort result retreats from maintenance to 180 days; not-demonstrated or did-not-recall still resets to one day. Maintenance never becomes permanent mastery.
